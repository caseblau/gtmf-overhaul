# Hot-seat co-op (one PC, two players)

Online play goes through MiusPlayer. This is the offline option: two people, one PC, no network, no code changes to the game.

## Setup
1. Quick battle, pick a *Duel* pool (module M3) or any battlegroup.
2. Agree the role split before deployment:
   - **Split A:** Player 1 commands all infantry, mortars and AT guns. Player 2 commands all armour and halftracks.
   - **Split B:** Player 1 is company commander (all manoeuvre units). Player 2 runs artillery, forward observers and reserves.
3. Agree an order cadence: pause every 3 minutes of game time (the clock top-left), or on every reinforcement arrival.

## Turn protocol
- The game runs paused between order phases.
- On each pause, Player 1 gives orders to their units, says "done", hands over the mouse. Player 2 does the same. Unpause.
- A unit may only be ordered by its owner. Attachments (a tank platoon attached to an infantry company) follow the owner of the parent formation unless both players agree otherwise at setup.
- Any pause outside the cadence is allowed for a casualty or a surprise, but both players get an order phase on it.

## Optional
- Chess clock: 60 seconds per order phase.
- Swap roles after the battle and replay the same pool and map to compare outcomes.
