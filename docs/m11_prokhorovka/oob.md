# M11 Prokhorovka — forces 11–13 July 1943 (research notes, 2026-10-22)

Figures with their sources; where sources disagree both are given. "Z&F" = Zetterling & Frankson, *Kursk 1943*;
"Lawrence" = C. Lawrence, *Kursk: The Battle of Prokhorovka* and the Dupuy Institute posts; "Zamulin" = V. Zamulin,
*Demolishing the Myth*; "Wheatley" = B. Wheatley, Journal of Intelligence History 2019/2021; "Nipe" = G. Nipe, *Blood,
Steel and Myth*; "Frieser" = GSWW vol. VIII.

## 1. German: II. SS-Panzerkorps (Hausser)

### Armour, serviceable
| | eve 11 July (Z&F) | eve 11 July (4. PzArmee / warhistory.org) | eve 13 July |
|---|---|---|---|
| LSSAH | 77 tanks+StuG, 4 Tigers | 56 tanks incl. 4 Tigers (+7 command tanks) | — |
| Das Reich | 95, 1 Tiger | 61 tanks incl. 8 captured T-34, 1 Tiger | — |
| Totenkopf | 122, 10 Tigers (Nipe, Clark) | 94 panzers, all ~10 Tigers | 49 operational, 0 Tigers |
| Corps | 294 incl. 15 Tigers | 211 (117 south of the Psel) | 251 (Z&F) |
(The two columns count differently: Z&F include StuG and command tanks; the Army figure is tanks only.)

Types: the mass is Pz IV lang (7.5 cm L/48) and Pz III lang (5 cm L/60); LSSAH 7 July return (Ballantyne via Dupuy):
67 Pz IV, 10 Pz III M, 3 Pz III J, plus the 13. (schwere) Kp with 4 serviceable Tigers on 12 July; StuG-Abt with ~30 StuG
III/StuH (35 on 30 June, OKH), Panzerjäger-Abt with ~20–29 Marder (OKH 30 June). Das Reich's Panzer-Regiment 2 fielded a
company of captured T-34s (8 serviceable on 11 July). Totenkopf's Tiger company: ~10–11 serviceable on the evening of
11 July, all out of action by 13 July (damage and breakdowns, Dupuy "Tank losses 12/13 July").

Losses 12 July: Wheatley (photo evidence) 5 Pz IV lost (LSSAH); Lawrence 19 (LSSAH); LSSAH 12 July report (via Dupuy
comments): 12 Pz IV, 1 Tiger, 11 StuG combat-damaged; destroyed/abandoned 2 Marder, 1 StuG, 4 Pz IV. Frieser: at most 3
total losses in the corps on 12 July. Corps personnel losses 12 July: 842 (Z&F).

### Formations and sectors
- **LSSAH (Wisch)**, centre between the Psel and the railway: SS-PzGrenRgt 1 (three battalions), SS-PzGrenRgt 2 (III. Btl
  armoured in SPW), SS-PzRgt 1 with the II. Abteilung (5., 6., 7. Kp) + 13. (schwere) Kp (Tigers; the I. Abt had left
  for the Panther conversion; Lawrence argues a provisional I. Abt existed), SS-StuG-Abt 1, SS-PzJg-Abt 1 (Marder),
  SS-PzAufkl-Abt 1 (Wittmann's Tigers attached to it on 12 July), SS-ArtRgt 1 (incl. Wespe/Hummel), Werfer-Abt, Flak-Abt
  (8.8 cm), Pionier-Btl. 11 July: took Oktyabrsky sovkhoz and Hill 252.2. 12 July: on the defensive at Oktyabrsky /
  252.2, counter-attacked in the afternoon.
- **SS-Totenkopf (Priess)**, north of the Psel (bridgehead at Krasny Oktyabr / Kozlovka, attacking towards Polezhaev and
  Hill 226.6): SS-PzGrenRgt 5 "Thule", SS-PzGrenRgt 6 "Theodor Eicke", SS-PzRgt 3 (both battalions + Tiger company),
  StuG-Abt 3, ArtRgt 3, Werfer, Flak. Attacked by Soviet infantry at 03:15 and 07:45 on 12 July, advanced along the
  north bank; pulled back from its gains on 13 July.
- **SS-Das Reich (Krüger)**, south of the railway (Yasnaya Polyana – Belenikhino – Storozhevoe): SS-PzGrenRgt
  "Deutschland", "Der Führer", SS-PzRgt 2 (incl. T-34 company, Tiger company with 1 serviceable), StuG-Abt 2, ArtRgt 2.
  Faced 2nd Guards TC and 2nd TC on 12 July; attacked towards Storozhevoe/Vinogradovka on 13 July.
- Corps: Werfer-Rgt 55, schwere Artillerie, Flak; Luftwaffe VIII. Fliegerkorps 654 sorties on 12 July (Bergström).

## 2. Soviet: 5th Guards Tank Army (Rotmistrov) and attachments

Army totals 12 July (Z&F, Glantz & House, Nipe): ~793 tanks + 37–57 SP guns (≈840 AFVs; Nipe 850). About two-thirds
T-34, the rest T-70, plus 31–35 Churchill Mk IV (36th and 47th Guards Heavy Tank Regiments). Attached: five artillery
regiments, one artillery brigade, one AA division. ~616 AFVs engaged against II SS-PzK on 12 July, ~500 in the first two
waves (430 + 70).

Corps total 16:00 11 July: 123 T-34, 81 T-70, 11 SU-122, 8 SU-76 (≈212 AFVs, matching Wikipedia's 212).
Losses 12 July: 32nd TBde 54 T-34 burnt/knocked out/repair, 100 KIA 130 WIA; 31st TBde 20 T-34 + 18 T-70; 25th TBde 13
T-34 + 10 T-70 lost, 11 + 10 knocked out/mined, 7 + 4 breakdowns, 140 KIA 180 WIA; 53rd MRBde 517 KIA/MIA 572 WIA;
1446th SAP 8 SU-122 + 3 SU-76 destroyed. Army report 07:00 13 July for the corps: 95 T-34, 38 T-70, 8 SP lost.
13 July 13:00 in line: 32nd 12 T-34; 31st 8 T-34 + 20 T-70. Rotmistrov: the corps lost 60 % of its armour on 12 July.

### 18th Tank Corps (Bakharov), north of the road to the Psel (Petrovka – Mikhailovka – Andreevka – Vasilyevka)
~190 tanks and SP guns on 12 July (Wikipedia/Z&F). 110th, 170th, 181st Tank Bdes, 32nd Motor Rifle Bde, 36th Guards
Heavy Tank Rgt (Churchill Mk IV; ~21 on strength, the army's 31–35 Churchills split with the 47th Gds HTR of 2 Gds TC),
292nd Mortar Rgt, 80th Guards Mortar Rgt (Katyusha), 100th AT Art Rgt, 76/57 mm AT regiments, recon detachment. Plan
of 12 July (corps account via Dupuy): first echelon 181st and 170th TBdes towards Malye Mayachki; second echelon 32nd
MRBde with 292nd Mortar Rgt and the AT regiments, 36th Gds HTR behind the 170th on the right flank; third echelon
110th TBde. By 12:00 the 170th had lost 60 % of its equipment at the ravine SE of Mikhailovka; the 181st reached
Andreevka–Hill 241.6 and Oktyabrsky by 14:00 (one of its battalions destroyed by Wittmann's Tigers); the 36th Gds HTR
was shot up by Tigers and AT guns 2 km south of Mikhailovka; evening line Vasilyevka–Mikhailovka–Prelestnoe–Petrovka.
Losses 12 July: 81 tanks (Lawrence); 11–14 July: 30 irrecoverable + 20 evacuated (corps report via Dupuy); Rotmistrov:
30 % of its armour.

### 2nd Guards Tank Corps (Burdeyny), south of the railway vs Das Reich
~120 tanks on 12 July. 4th, 25th, 26th Guards Tank Bdes, 4th Guards Motor Rifle Bde, 47th Guards Heavy Tank Rgt
(Churchill), 1500th AT Rgt, mortar regiment. The 26th Gds TBde (~40 tanks) was detached south against III. PzK later on
12 July. Attack axis Belenikhino – Yasnaya Polyana – Kalinin.

### 2nd Tank Corps (Popov), remnants in support of 2 Gds TC
26th, 99th, 169th Tank Bdes, 58th Motor Rifle Bde; worn down since 6 July, no reliable strength on 12 July (reinforced by
the 9th Gds Airborne Div on 11 July). Treat as a weak corps (~50–80 AFVs) pending Zamulin's figures.

### 5th Guards Mechanised Corps (Skvortsov), second echelon NW of Prokhorovka
~113 tanks and SP guns on 12 July. 10th, 11th, 12th Guards Mechanised Bdes, 24th Guards Tank Bde, 1447th SAP. The
11th and 12th Gds MBdes and the army's 53rd Guards Tank Rgt went south with Trufanov's group against III. PzK on 12 July;
the 10th Gds MBde and 24th Gds TBde attacked Totenkopf on 13 July.

### Rifle and airborne formations (5th Guards Army, Zhadov; 33rd Guards Rifle Corps)
- 9th Guards Airborne Div: Prokhorovka and the ground between the Psel and the railway; its battalions rode the tanks
  of 18th and 29th TC on 12 July.
- 42nd Guards Rifle Div: Prelestnoe – Petrovka – Beregovoe, flank of 18th TC.
- 95th Guards Rifle Div and 52nd Guards Rifle Div (23rd Gds RC, depleted): Psel bend against Totenkopf (Polezhaev,
  Vasilyevka, Hill 226.6); 301st AT Art Rgt from 33rd Gds RC.
- 183rd Rifle Div (48th RC): with 2nd Gds TC south of the railway.
- Army artillery: five artillery regiments + one brigade with 5th GTA (incl. 678th Howitzer Rgt), 1529th Heavy SP Rgt
  (SU-152; appears in one 29th TC report where the 1446th is expected), Guards Mortar (Katyusha) regiments, AA division.

Losses 12–16 July (5th GTA HQ report 17 July, via Zamulin): 222 T-34, 89 T-70, 12 Churchill, 8 SU-122, 3 SU-76
irrecoverable; 143 T-34, 56 T-70, 7 Churchill, 3 SU-122, 3 SU-76 under repair; 2,940 KIA, 3,510 WIA, 1,157 MIA.
Zamulin (2017), 12 July alone: 340 AFVs damaged or destroyed, 3,563 personnel. Lawrence: 240 tanks (18 TC 81, 29 TC 159).

## 3. What this means for the operation (11–13 July, 18 turns of 4 h)

- Turn 1 (11 July 06:00): LSSAH attack on Oktyabrsky / 252.2 against 9 Gds Airborne and 2 TC remnants; 18 TC and 29 TC
  arrive in the Prokhorovka area during 11 July (arrival turns 2–4); TK in the Psel bridgehead vs 95/52 Gds RD; DR vs
  2 Gds TC / 183 RD. Turn 7 (12 July 06:00): Soviet general attack. 5 Gds MC (10 Gds MBde, 24 Gds TBde) arrives as
  reserve turn 7–9; Trufanov's detachment leaves the map (11/12 Gds MBde not deployed). 13 July: TK withdrawal, DR push.
- Engine fit: LSSAH = clone of the Mius-Front Das Reich structure (30 July file) with II./PzRgt 1 (Pz IV, Pz III), Tiger
  platoon, StuG battery, Marder company, SPW battalion; Soviet tank brigades as 1 T-34 bn + 1 T-70 bn (25th, 31st) or
  2 T-34 bns (32nd), motor rifle brigades, SU-76/SU-122 regiment; Churchill regiments need the Churchill Mk IV in
  `div_units_ussr` (to check), else substitute KV-1S.
- Strength basis: evening 11 July returns for the Germans (LSSAH 56–77 AFVs incl. 4 Tigers, DR 61–95, TK 94–122 with 10
  Tigers), 08:00 11 July returns for 29 TC, ~190 for 18 TC, ~120 for 2 Gds TC, ~113 for 5 Gds MC.
- Open: brigade-level figures for 18 TC, 2 Gds TC, 5 Gds MC and the rifle divisions' strengths (Zamulin's tables, pamyat-
  naroda.ru documents); per-type LSSAH/TK/DR returns for 11 and 12 July (NARA T313 / Wheatley 2021 tables).
