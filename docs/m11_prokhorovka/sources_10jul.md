# 10 July 1943 start: positions, strengths and arrival schedule (sources)

Companion of `sources_11_14jul.md`; the layout is `layout_10jul.json` (DEM-lattice metres, shifted into the 7 x 5 block
by `tools/prokh_op.py`). Page numbers are those of the PDF of Zamulin, *Demolishing the Myth* (Helion 2011) and of
Lawrence, *The Battle of Prokhorovka* (Stackpole 2019).

## German side, 06:00 10 July
- Operational Order No. 17 of the LSSAH (signed 22:15 9 July, Zamulin pp. 168-169): the reinforced 2 SS-PzGr Rgt (StuG
  bn, Tiger company, a pioneer company, 5./Flak) attacks at 06:00 after the barrage of the whole 1 SS-AR and Werfer Rgt
  55 along the Teterevino - Prokhorovka road; the reinforced recon bn follows through the breach past the northern tip
  of the Komsomolets wood to svkh. Oktyabrsky and Hill 252.4; the Pz Rgt (minus Tigers) stands ready S of the Teterevino
  - Luchki road. Boundary to Das Reich: Teterevino - the wood E of Ivanovsky Vyselok - Storozhevoye - Yamki - 230.5;
  to Totenkopf: Hill 254.5 (500 m N of Teterevino) - Vasilyevka - the Psel villages (T).
- Totenkopf: before dawn PzGr crossed the Psel at Krasny Oktyabr to form a bridgehead; at 05:30 a reconnaissance in
  force (a PzGr bn and ten tanks) on Vasilyevka / Kozlovka; at 16:00, after a strike by 80 bombers, the attack out of
  the bridgehead on Klyuchi and the south slopes of 226.6; by 20:00 Gruppe Becker 800 m N of the branch, Gruppe Baum
  in the wood at Klyuchi; the 52 gds RD counter-attack at 21:00 with the training bn and a company of 245 TR; the
  bridgehead (Krasny Oktyabr - Klyuchi - S slopes of 226.6) held through the night (Zamulin pp. 181, 187-188).
- Das Reich: from Teterevino / Yasnaya Polyana against Ivanovsky Vyselok, the Storozhevoye wood and Yamki
  (Zamulin p. 185; Lawrence "Das Reich Resumes the Advance, 10 July", p. 159).
- Armour at 19:05 9 July (Zetterling / Frankson via Zamulin Table 11, p. 166): LSSAH 4 Pz II, 4 Pz III, 32 Pz IV,
  4 Tiger, 5 command, 22 StuG = 71; DR 31 Pz III, 13 Pz IV, 1 Tiger, 7 T-34, 7 command, 26 StuG = 85; T 47 Pz III,
  20 Pz IV L43/48 + 7 L24, 2 Tiger, 5 command, 12 StuG = 93.  Lawrence counts 94 / 98 / 105 for the 10 July engagements
  (pp. 156-159).
- Weather 10 July: heavy showers until about 14:00, clearing after 15:00, roads sodden (Lawrence p. 157); LSSAH had
  no air support that day, Totenkopf 62 sorties (p. 156).

## Soviet side, 06:00 10 July
- 285 RR of 183 RD (Lt Col A. K. Karpov) in the rear army belt Vasilyevka - svkh. Komsomolets - Hill 258.2 (excl.) -
  Ivanovsky Vyselok (1 and 2 bns with II/623 AR), 3 bn with 169 TBr at Storozhevoye (Zamulin pp. 175-177; strength
  1,748 men, 6 x 45 mm, 4 x 76 mm AT, 23 x 82 mm and 5 x 120 mm mortars on 30 June).
- 2 TC (Maj Gen A. F. Popov), subordinated to 69 A from 00:00 10 July (Private Combat Order No. 00967/OP, p. 179):
  99 TBr dug in at Vasilyevka, Andreyevka (2 TB on the NW edge) and Mikhailovka (1 TB in reserve) with 1 and 2 bns of
  11 MRBr (10 TC) and two batteries of 1502 IPTAP; 26 TBr dug in since 8 July on the line Mikhailovka - Stalinskoye
  otd. of the Oktyabrsky sovkhoz, 500 m from the embankment; 169 TBr at Storozhevoye, its 370 TB dug in in the grove at
  Ivanovsky Vyselok with batteries of 1502 IPTAP and I/269 MTR, 371 TB in the wood N of the sovkhoz; 15 gds separate
  heavy TR (Churchill, 12 runners) at Storozhevoye (pp. 176-177, 183-186).  Runners at 08:00 10 July: 116 tanks in the
  corps, 89 in the three brigades (43 T-70): 26 TBr 58 %, 99 and 169 TBr 55 % of establishment (p. 180).
- 11 MRBr of 10 TC: 3 bn dug in on the crest of 226.6, 1 and 2 bns at Vasilyevka (p. 175).
- 52 gds RD (Lt Col G. G. Pantyukhov) on the north bank: 151 gds RR from the knoll 500 m N of Klyuchi to 1.5 km SW of
  226.6, 155 gds RR between 226.6 and the grove SE of Klyuchi, 153 gds RR from the path 1.5 km E of 226.6 to
  Polezhayev; 124 gds AR 1 km SW of Polezhayev and 400 m SE of Vesyoly (p. 175).
- 2 gds TC (Col A. S. Burdeiny): relieved by 93 gds RD by 05:00 10 July and moved to Belenikhino - Leski - Shakhovo -
  Dalny Dolzhik, HQ Plota (Order No. 00969/OP, p. 179); 141 runners, brigades at 88-89 % (p. 180).
- 27 AT Bde (Front reserve, Col V. A. Malyshkov): batteries firing from Prokhorovka station, Yamki, Grushki and
  Storozhevoye on 10 July (p. 185); the Front's sapper AT reserve mined the Komsomolets - station axis.
- 5 gds TA: ordered on the evening of 9 July to put 18 TC on the north bank Vesyoly - Polezhayev - Prelestnoye -
  Aleksandrovsky as a second echelon and two brigades of 5 gds MC at the Zapselets; its main body assembled at
  Bobryshevo - Srednyaya Olshanka - Maryino - Prokhorovka by the end of 9 July and was held back (pp. 172-173).  In the
  operation 18 TC and 29 TC enter in the evening of 11 July (turns 10-11), 5 gds MC on 12 July (turns 12-13), as they
  were committed historically.
- 5 gds A: Combat Order No. 16/OP of 14:30 10 July - the divisions are to be on the line by 04:00 11 July (p. 173);
  9 gds AB Div marched into Prokhorovka at 22:10 10 July (p. 198); 95 gds RD took over the Psel bend from 52 gds RD
  on the night of 10/11 July, 42 gds RD the Petrovka - Prelestnoye sector on 11 July.

## Course of 10 July (for the briefings)
- 10:45 the LSSAH attack: I/2 SS-PzGr Rgt on the SW edge of the sovkhoz, II/2 along the Teterevino - Ivanovsky Vyselok
  road to the railway bend, III/2 in reserve on half-tracks at Teterevino; 11:00 the counter-attack of 99 TBr and
  11 MRBr drove Totenkopf from Kozlovka and the W edge of Vasilyevka; 13:00 the recon bn into the gap, 13:30 the
  counter-attack of 15 gds hvy TR from Storozhevoye (12 Churchills, one runner left); 14:20 LSSAH on the crest of
  241.6, the hill fought over two more hours; by 16:00 the sovkhoz and Ivanovsky Vyselok lost, 285 RR falling back on
  Vasilyevka and Storozhevoye; 26 TBr ordered to 241.6 from Stalinskoye otd.; LSSAH stopped on the E and NE edges of
  the sovkhoz by the flanking fire from the Psel villages and the 27 AT Bde batteries (Zamulin pp. 181-186).
